# Scalable Diffusion Models with Transformers
**Authors, venue, year:** William Peebles (UC Berkeley; work done during an internship at Meta AI FAIR), Saining Xie (New York University); arXiv 2212.09748 (v2, 2 Mar 2023); ICCV 2023 (venue from background knowledge, not in the extracted text) · **Key:** `dit2022` · **OpenAlex citations:** 9650 · **Era:** df
**Read status:** full text (main paper and the appendix tables/model details)

## 1. Problem and motivation
Transformers had taken over language, vision and other domains, yet image diffusion models all still used the convolutional U-Net inherited from PixelCNN++ (Sec. 1). Architecture studies for diffusion (Dhariwal & Nichol) kept the U-Net's high-level design, so it was unknown whether its inductive bias mattered and whether diffusion backbones obey the clean scaling behavior seen in other transformer models.

## 2. Core idea in one paragraph
Replace the U-Net with a plain Vision Transformer that operates on patches of a latent image, inside the latent diffusion framework (`ldm2021`). Cut the latent into patches, embed them as tokens, run standard transformer blocks, and inject the timestep and class label through adaptive layer norm whose output gates are initialized to zero, so every block starts as the identity ("adaLN-Zero"). Then measure scale not by parameters but by forward-pass Gflops: models with more Gflops, from larger width/depth or from more tokens via smaller patches, give consistently better FID. Analogy: the same recipe that scaled language models, applied to denoising.

## 3. The mathematics
**(a) Forward and training objective (Sec. 3.1)**
$$x_t=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\epsilon_t,\qquad L_{simple}(\theta)=\|\epsilon_\theta(x_t)-\epsilon_t\|_2^2$$
| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $x_0$ | clean data; here the VAE latent $z=E(x)$ |
| $\bar\alpha_t$ | cumulative noise-schedule constants (hyperparameters) |
| $\epsilon_t\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$ | noise added |
| $\epsilon_\theta$ | the DiT, predicting the noise |

The covariance $\Sigma_\theta$ is learned with the full variational term following Nichol & Dhariwal: $\epsilon_\theta$ trained with $L_{simple}$, $\Sigma_\theta$ with the full $L$. Meaning: same objective as `ddpm2020`; the paper's novelty is the backbone.

**(b) Classifier-free guidance (Sec. 3.1)**
$$\hat\epsilon_\theta(x_t,c)=\epsilon_\theta(x_t,\varnothing)+s\cdot\big(\epsilon_\theta(x_t,c)-\epsilon_\theta(x_t,\varnothing)\big)$$
where $c$ is the class label, $\varnothing$ a learned null embedding (obtained by randomly dropping $c$ in training), and $s>1$ the guidance scale ($s=1$ recovers standard sampling). This is the guidance of `cfg2022` written with scale $s=1+w$.

**(c) Tokens and compute (Sec. 3.2, Fig. 4)**
$$T=(I/p)^2,$$
where $I\times I\times C$ is the latent size (32x32x4 for 256x256 images), $p$ the patch size, $T$ the number of tokens. Meaning: halving $p$ quadruples $T$ and at least quadruples Gflops but barely changes parameters, so Gflops can be scaled independently of parameter count. Training compute is estimated as model Gflops x batch size x steps x 3 (Sec. 5).

**(d) adaLN-Zero (Sec. 3.2)** The layer-norm scale and shift $\gamma,\beta$ and residual gates $\alpha$ are regressed by an MLP from the sum of the timestep and class embeddings; the MLP is zero-initialized for all $\alpha$ so each block starts as identity. (No numbered equation; described in prose.)

## 4. Method / architecture / algorithm
1. Encode image with a frozen off-the-shelf Stable Diffusion VAE (downsample factor 8): 256x256x3 to 32x32x4.
2. Patchify with patch size $p\in\{2,4,8\}$ to $T$ tokens of width $d$, add sine-cosine positional embeddings.
3. $N$ DiT blocks (adaLN-Zero chosen over in-context, cross-attention and plain adaLN variants); final adaptive layer norm and linear decoder to $p\times p\times 2C$ (noise and diagonal covariance), reshaped to the latent grid.
4. Four sizes (Table 1): S (12 layers, d=384, 6 heads), B (12, 768, 12), L (24, 1024, 16), XL (28, 1152, 16); XL/2 has 118.6 Gflops and 675M parameters (Table 4).
5. Training: AdamW, constant lr $10^{-4}$, no weight decay, batch size 256, horizontal flip only, EMA 0.9999, no warmup or regularization; ADM's diffusion setup with 1000-step linear schedule $10^{-4}$ to $2\times10^{-2}$; JAX on TPU v3 pods (XL/2 about 5.7 iterations/s on a v3-256).

## 5. Achievements
- Conditioning ablation (Fig. 5, Table 4): at 400K steps, FID-50K for XL/2 is 19.47 (adaLN-Zero) vs 25.21 (adaLN), 26.14 (cross-attention), 35.24 (in-context).
- Scaling (Figs. 2, 6, 8): across 12 models, FID correlates with Gflops (correlation -0.93, Fig. 8); S/8 153.60 to XL/2 19.47 at 400K steps (Table 4).
- ImageNet 256x256 (Table 2): DiT-XL/2-G (cfg=1.50) FID 2.27, IS 278.24, precision 0.83, recall 0.57, beating LDM-4-G 3.60, ADM-G 4.59, StyleGAN-XL 2.30; without guidance FID 9.62; trained 7M steps; at 2.35M steps FID 2.55 (Sec. 5.1).
- ImageNet 512x512 (Table 3): FID 3.04 (cfg=1.50) vs ADM-G,ADM-U 3.85 and StyleGAN-XL 2.41 (StyleGAN-XL is lower on this table).
- Compute efficiency: 118.6 Gflops vs LDM-4 103.6, ADM 1120, ADM-U 742 Gflops at 256 (Sec. 5.1); at 512, 524.6 vs ADM 1983 and ADM-U 2813.
- Sampling compute cannot substitute for model compute (Fig. 10): XL/2 at 128 steps (15.2 Tflops) FID-10K 23.7 beats L/2 at 1000 steps (80.7 Tflops) 25.9.

## 6. Limitations
**Stated by the authors:** the paper has no dedicated limitations section; it notes conclusions are for class-conditional ImageNet in a pretrained VAE's latent space, leaves scaling to larger models/token counts and text-to-image as future work, and uses guidance only on the first three latent channels (appendix; 3-channel scale 1.5 gives 2.27, 4-channel 1.375 gives 2.20).
**Seen in hindsight (hindsight):** FID-vs-Gflops evidence is limited to ImageNet with a fixed VAE; the VAE decoder remains a ceiling; the study did not include text conditioning, where later work had to add cross-attention or joint attention (`sd3_2024`).

## 7. Place in the lineage
- **Builds on:** `ldm2021` (latent framework and VAE), `ddpm2020`, `cfg2022`, `vit2020` (ViT blocks, size configs), `transformer2017`, ADM (Dhariwal & Nichol), adaLN in GAN/U-Net diffusion, the language-model scaling results the paper cites (cf. `scaling2020`).
- **Led to:** `sd3_2024` (MM-DiT), `cosmos2025`, video/world-model DiTs, and later diffusion-transformer image/video backbones (hindsight).
- **Single idea added:** a diffusion backbone can be a plain ViT with adaLN-Zero conditioning, and its quality tracks forward-pass Gflops predictably.
