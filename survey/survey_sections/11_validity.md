## Threats to validity

- **Selection.** The 65 papers were curated from the author's knowledge of the field and ranked by citation count; they were not found by a systematic search. Influential papers outside the list (for example in classical reinforcement learning, in speech, or in other generative families) are absent. The older lines have more papers per year simply because they were chosen first.
- **Citation data.** OpenAlex counts the preprint and the journal version of a paper as separate records, so the counts for papers read from arXiv can be lower than other databases. Counts also depend on indexing and change over time. Links in the graph exist only between papers in the set.
- **Reading notes.** Notes were written by language-model agents from extracted PDF text, not by domain experts, and they have not been independently checked by a second reader. Equations were often garbled by extraction and were reconstructed from surrounding text; every such case is listed in the note's verification section. Several appendices, proofs and per-task result tables were not read; each note says which. Quoted numbers come from the text, but a transcription error is possible.
- **Hindsight links.** Lineage links added from the notes (93 of the 344 links) include judgements by the note writers about later influence. They are marked in the notes as hindsight and are weaker evidence than a citation link.
- **Internal inconsistencies of the papers.** Several papers disagree with themselves (for example differing BLEU, MMLU, speed-up and step-count figures between abstract, text and tables). The notes quote the table values and flag the difference. We have not tried to resolve them.
- **Recency.** The most recent papers (2024–2025) are the least studied and, in this set, the least cited. Statements about them should be read as provisional.
