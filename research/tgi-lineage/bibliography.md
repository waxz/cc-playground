# Bibliography

One row per report in [`papers/`](papers/) (92 reports). Rows are grouped by the study's groups (see [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md)) and sorted by year within each group. The table was generated by a script from the header table of each paper report and then checked by hand; where a header records a later journal version or the arXiv version read, the venue column keeps that information. "Source" records whether the report's author read the full text (`full text`) or worked from citing papers and established knowledge (`secondary`); the TGI entry was analysed from its code and README, since no paper exists. Paper full texts are not part of the repository.

Counts: 92 reports, of which 13 are secondary-source (`reinforce`, `pg-theorem`, `natural-pg`, `dpg`, `dyna`, `pilco`, `supertrack`, `raibert`, `kajita-zmp-preview`, `capture-point`, `ilqr-online-to`, `mujoco`, `convex-mpc-cheetah`), one is code-only (`tgi`), and 78 rest on a full-text reading. Groups g0–g7 contain 5, 12, 18, 13, 10, 14, 10 and 10 reports respectively, including depth-3 corpus additions.
