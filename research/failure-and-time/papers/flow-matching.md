## 1. Problem and position in the lineage

Continuous normalising flows (CNFs) transport a simple distribution to the data distribution by integrating a learned velocity field, but maximum-likelihood training requires simulating the ODE and is too expensive at scale. Flow Matching (FM) trains CNFs *simulation-free* by regressing the velocity field of a prescribed probability path, and it shows that diffusion paths are one member of a larger family that also contains straight optimal-transport (OT) paths. Together with [rectified flow](rectified-flow.md), FM is the objective of the current generation of video world models: [Cosmos3](cosmos3.md), [DreamDojo](dreamdojo.md) (via Cosmos-Predict2.5), [LingBot-VA](lingbot-va.md) and [WorldReforge](worldreforge.md) (its reference [30]) all regress the velocity $v^*=\epsilon-z_0$.

## 2. Method

Let $p_t$ be a probability path from $p_0=\mathcal N(0,I)$ to $p_1\approx q$ generated by a vector field $u_t$. The Flow Matching loss $\mathcal L_{FM}=\mathbb E_{t,p_t(x)}\Vert v_\theta(x,t)-u_t(x)\Vert^2$ is intractable because $u_t$ is a marginal over data. FM constructs $p_t$ as a mixture of per-sample conditional paths $p_t(x\mid x_1)$ with conditional fields $u_t(x\mid x_1)$, and proves that the **Conditional Flow Matching** loss
$$\mathcal L_{CFM}=\mathbb E_{t,\,q(x_1),\,p_t(x\mid x_1)}\big\Vert v_\theta(x,t)-u_t(x\mid x_1)\big\Vert^2$$
has the same gradient as $\mathcal L_{FM}$, so one can regress per-sample targets. For Gaussian conditional paths $p_t(x\mid x_1)=\mathcal N(\mu_t(x_1),\sigma_t(x_1)^2I)$ the conditional field is $u_t(x\mid x_1)=\frac{\sigma_t'}{\sigma_t}(x-\mu_t)+\mu_t'$. Choosing diffusion schedules recovers (variance-preserving or -exploding) diffusion paths; choosing $\mu_t=tx_1$, $\sigma_t=1-(1-\sigma_{\min})t$ gives the **OT path**, whose conditional flow $\psi_t(x_0)=(1-(1-\sigma_{\min})t)x_0+tx_1$ moves each noise sample in a straight line at constant speed, with target $u_t=x_1-(1-\sigma_{\min})x_0$. Sampling integrates $dx/dt=v_\theta(x,t)$ with an off-the-shelf ODE solver.

## 3. Evidence

On CIFAR-10 and downsampled ImageNet (32, 64 and 128 pixels), FM with OT paths gives better likelihood (bits/dim) and FID than FM with diffusion paths and than score-matching diffusion baselines trained in the same architecture, and requires fewer function evaluations (NFE) at sampling time because the learned trajectories are straighter. FM with diffusion paths is itself more stable than score matching. The paper also shows conditional OT paths producing near-linear sample trajectories in 2D, where diffusion paths curve and overshoot.

## 4. Critical analysis

FM's main contribution is conceptual unification: in Gaussian paths, $\epsilon$-, $x_0$-, $v$- and velocity-prediction are affine re-parameterisations of one another, and the training objectives differ only in how they weight noise levels (theme report, Mathematical background). Straight *conditional* paths do not imply straight *marginal* paths; the marginal field still averages over many data points and trajectories curve where those conditional paths cross, which is why [rectified flow](rectified-flow.md) proposes reflow and why large models still need timestep re-weighting ([SD3](sd3.md)). The paper's experiments are unconditional images; conditioning (text, frames, actions) is added in later work by feeding the condition to $v_\theta$, leaving the CFM theorem intact. Note the sign and direction conventions: FM integrates from noise at $t=0$ to data at $t=1$, whereas Cosmos3 and WorldReforge write $z_\sigma=(1-\sigma)z_0+\sigma\epsilon$ with data at $\sigma=0$ and target $v^*=\epsilon-z_0$; the objectives are identical up to the substitution $\sigma=1-t$ and a sign. In WorldReforge the linearity of this path is exactly what yields $\hat z_0=z_\sigma-\sigma v_\theta$ and hence its Interaction Alignment identity.

## 5. Cross-references

**Builds on.** CNFs (Chen et al., 2018), denoising score matching, [DDPM](ddpm.md).
**Extended by.** [SD3](sd3.md) scales rectified-flow/FM training with logit-normal timestep sampling; [Cosmos3](cosmos3.md), [LingBot-VA](lingbot-va.md) and [DreamDojo](dreamdojo.md) train video–action models with it; [WorldReforge](worldreforge.md) adds region and correspondence losses on top.
**Contrasts with.** [Rectified flow](rectified-flow.md), developed concurrently, reaches the same straight-path objective from a transport perspective and adds reflow.

## 6. Relevance to the study question

Question 1: FM supplies the base objective $\mathcal L_{FM}$ of current world models and the algebra that relates WorldReforge's $x_0$-space auxiliary loss to the velocity loss. Its continuous-time formulation is also a point of contact with the ODE-based time models of [t3](../themes/t3-time-embedding.md), though there the ODE runs over physical time rather than noise level.
