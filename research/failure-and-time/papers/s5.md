## 1. Problem and position in the lineage

[S4](s4.md) runs $H$ independent single-input single-output SSMs through FFT convolutions, which requires a fixed step across the sequence. S5 replaces them with one multi-input multi-output diagonal SSM computed by a parallel associative scan in the time domain. Because the scan does not need a time-invariant kernel, the step can vary per element, which makes S5 the first member of the diagonal-SSM line that natively handles irregular sampling. Of all prior work, it is the closest to [DAWIN](dawin.md).

## 2. Method

Diagonalise $A=V\Lambda V^{-1}$, set $\tilde B=V^{-1}B$, $\tilde C=CV$, and discretise by ZOH with learnable timescales $\Delta\in\mathbb R^P$:

$$\bar\Lambda=e^{\Lambda\Delta},\qquad \bar B=\Lambda^{-1}(\bar\Lambda-I)\tilde B,\qquad x_k=\bar\Lambda x_{k-1}+\bar Bu_k,\qquad y_k=\tilde Cx_k+Du_k.$$

The recurrence is the scan of the associative operator $(a_i,b_i)\bullet(a_j,b_j)=(a_ja_i,\;a_jb_i+b_j)$ over elements $(\bar\Lambda,\bar Bu_k)$, with $O(P\log L)$ parallel depth and $O(PL)$ work for diagonal $\bar\Lambda$. For irregular data, a different $\Delta t_k$ is supplied at each step, so $\bar\Lambda_k=e^{\Lambda\Delta t_k}$ and $\bar B_k$ vary. Initialisation uses the diagonalisable HiPPO-N (skew-symmetric) part; conjugate symmetry halves the state. Section 4 proves that under tied timescales and a specific $B$ the S5 output equals a linear combination of S4 SSMs.

## 3. Evidence

LRA average 87.46, Path-X 98.58 % (Table 1). Speech Commands 35-way raw audio: 96.52 % at 16 kHz and 94.53 % at 8 kHz zero-shot by rescaling $\Delta$, vs S4-LegS 96.08 / 91.32 and ConvNet 95.51 / 7.26 (Table 2). Pendulum regression from irregularly sampled, noise-corrupted images (50 frames, $T=100$): MSE ×10⁻³ 3.41 ± 0.27 vs CRU 3.94 (authors' run), RKN-Δt 5.09, ODE-RNN 7.26, mTAND 65.64, at 86× the speed of CRU (Table 3).

## 4. Critical analysis

S5 is the evidence that a linear time-varying recurrence with exact exponential transitions handles variable $\Delta t$ well, at negligible cost, on a nonlinear physical system (the pendulum), though the nonlinearity is supplied by the stacked layers, not by the SSM. The pendulum task has a single irregular-sampling benchmark and its intervals are drawn from the same distribution in training and test, so extrapolation in $\Delta t$ is tested only by the 16→8 kHz experiment, where $\Delta$ is rescaled uniformly. ZOH assumes the input is constant over each interval; for sampled observations this is a modelling choice and its error grows with $\Delta t$ relative to the input bandwidth. The paper's own conclusion points to time-varying and input-dependent SSMs (Liquid-S4, later [Mamba](mamba.md)). Relative to [DAWIN](dawin.md): S5's input matrix is a dense learnable $\tilde B$ applied to linear inputs, with the exact complex factor $\Lambda^{-1}(e^{\Lambda\Delta}-I)$; DAWIN replaces $\tilde Bu$ by a diagonal bank of sinusoidal features and the factor by its real-pole numerator. The associative operator DAWIN proves in its Proposition 2 is S5's operator.
